## Terms this site uses

<span id="definition"></span>**Definition.** A text that says what counts as an AI incident, such as the [OECD](/rival-ai-incident-definitions/definitions/oecd/) or [EU AI Act](/rival-ai-incident-definitions/definitions/eu-ai-act/) definition.

<span id="coding"></span>**Database or coding.** A record of actual incidents with labels attached, such as the [AI Incident Database](/rival-ai-incident-definitions/definitions/aiid/) or [CSET](/rival-ai-incident-definitions/definitions/cset/)'s annotations of it.

<span id="rule"></span>**Rule.** A definition written as a test on a database's fields, so it can be applied to every coded incident. The OECD definition is applied to CSET's data through a rule that reads CSET's fields.

<span id="reading"></span>**Reading.** One defensible way to interpret part of a definition. The OECD's rights clause can be read narrowly (legal rights violations only) or broadly (also hate speech and misinformation). See [Readings](/rival-ai-incident-definitions/readings/).

<span id="headline"></span>**Headline reading.** The reading a result is first reported under, chosen by a fixed rule: the reading the source's own text supports; where the source is silent, the most literal reading; where nothing decides, both.

<span id="three-state"></span>**Three-state label.** Every rule answers yes, no, or can't tell for each incident. "Can't tell" covers blanks, "maybe" and "unclear" codes.

<span id="assignment"></span>**Assignment.** Filling in every "can't tell" as yes or no. The **assignment range** is the lowest and highest a result can be over every assignment; a result that holds at every assignment does not depend on how the unknowns resolve.

<span id="crosswalk"></span>**Crosswalk.** A table of the same incidents classified two ways, showing which incidents each side counts and where they disagree.

<span id="kappa"></span>**Cohen's kappa (κ).** Agreement between two classifications after removing the agreement expected by chance: 1 is perfect agreement, 0 is chance level.

<span id="near-miss"></span>**Near miss.** An event where harm nearly occurred. Sources draw this line differently; compare [CSET](/rival-ai-incident-definitions/definitions/cset/) and the [OECD](/rival-ai-incident-definitions/definitions/oecd/).

<span id="preregistration"></span>**Preregistration.** Writing down the hypotheses, rules and pass/fail thresholds, and freezing them, before any result is computed.
