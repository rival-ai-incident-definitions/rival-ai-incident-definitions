# MIT AI Incident Tracker

The [MIT AI Incident Tracker](https://airisk.mit.edu/blog/ai-incident-tracker-june-2026-update), from MIT FutureTech and the MIT AI Risk Repository, classifies the incidents in the [AI Incident Database](/rival-ai-incident-definitions/definitions/aiid/) with language models rather than human coders.

## Method

> The AI Incident Tracker uses large language models (LLMs) to process incident reports from the AI Incident Database (AIID) and classify them along five taxonomies, producing standardized data that policymakers and researchers can use to track trends.

One of those taxonomies grades seriousness: "Harm Severity (how serious the harm was, rated across 10 harm-types)".

| Element | Text |
|---|---|
| Who codes | Large language models |
| What is coded | Incidents already admitted by the AI Incident Database |
| AI involvement | Not stated; the tracker classifies incidents the database has already admitted |
| Harm that did not occur | Not stated |
| Seriousness | Graded, "rated across 10 harm-types" |

## Role in the Study

The study compares the tracker's language-model labels with CSET's human annotations on the same incidents, without scoring either as correct; see the [Study Overview](/rival-ai-incident-definitions/study/).
