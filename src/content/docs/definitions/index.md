Four sources define what counts as an AI incident. Each page quotes the definition and its commentary from the source.

| source | term | AI involvement | harm that did not occur |
|---|---|---|---|
| [OECD](/rival-ai-incident-definitions/definitions/oecd/) | AI incident; AI hazard | "directly or indirectly leads to" | a separate term, AI hazard, which includes near misses |
| [EU AI Act](/rival-ai-incident-definitions/definitions/eu-ai-act/) | serious incident | "directly or indirectly leads to" | no provision in Article 3(49) |
| [CSET](/rival-ai-incident-definitions/definitions/cset/) | AI tangible harm event; special interest intangible AI harm | "can be directly linked to"; at least a but-for cause | tangible: separate near-miss and issue levels; intangible: included as risk |
| [AI Incident Database](/rival-ai-incident-definitions/definitions/aiid/) | AI incident | "implicated"; at least a but-for cause | included: "near harm" |
