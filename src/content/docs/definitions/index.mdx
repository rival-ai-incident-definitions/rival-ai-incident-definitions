Two kinds of source decide what counts as an AI incident. A **definition** is a text that says what counts. A **database or coding** records actual incidents and labels them, and both kinds are compared on the same rows. Each page quotes its source; a cell reads "not yet sourced" until its source is retrieved and the quotation verified.
