# CSETv0

Before its current taxonomy, CSET coded AI Incident Database incidents with its first edition, [CSETv0](https://incidentdatabase.ai/taxonomies/csetv0/), and 100 incidents carry codes under both.

> Every incident is independently classified by two CSET annotators. Annotations are peer reviewed and finally randomly selected for quality control ahead of publication.

The first edition asks one question about harm, "Was harm caused, or was it a near miss?", records "the type(s) of harm caused or nearly caused by the incident", and has an explicit value for uncertain cases: "\"Unclear\" applies if the evidence is contradictory or too thin to apply either of the above labels."

| Element | CSETv0 | [CSETv1](/rival-ai-incident-definitions/definitions/cset/) |
|---|---|---|
| Harm that did not occur | A near miss is one answer to the harm question | Separate near-miss and issue levels for tangible harm |
| Uncertain cases | An "Unclear" value | Coders take the lowest clearly known level |

Source: AI Incident Database, *CSETv0 taxonomy*. [incidentdatabase.ai](https://incidentdatabase.ai/taxonomies/csetv0/)
